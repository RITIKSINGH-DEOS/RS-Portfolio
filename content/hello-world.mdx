# My Journey as a Full Stack Developer

Hey 👋, I'm Ritik Singh, a B.Tech CSE student and Full Stack Developer passionate about building modern web applications and exploring Artificial Intelligence.

My journey started with learning the fundamentals of web development. Over time, I mastered the MERN stack and began building real-world applications that solve practical problems. From authentication systems and dashboards to AI-powered applications, every project helped me improve my problem-solving and development skills.

## Building Real-World Projects

I enjoy creating applications that combine clean UI with powerful backend functionality. Some of my favorite projects include:

- **Resume AI** – An AI-powered resume builder with intelligent content generation.
- **MedConnect** – A healthcare platform connecting patients and doctors.
- **FlowFunds** – A modern finance tracker for managing expenses and budgets.
- **AI Learning Assistant** – A MERN application that lets students upload documents, generate AI summaries, chat with PDFs, create flashcards, and take AI-powered quizzes using Google Gemini API.
- **Support AI** – An AI customer support platform that enables businesses to create custom chatbots and embed them into their websites.

## Tech Stack

## Current Focus

Currently, I'm expanding my knowledge in AI-powered web development by learning technologies like LangChain, LangGraph, Ollama, and TensorFlow while continuing to build scalable full-stack applications.

## Looking Ahead

My goal is to become a Full Stack AI Developer who builds intelligent products that combine modern web technologies with Artificial Intelligence to solve real-world problems.
